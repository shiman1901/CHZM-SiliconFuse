Reading the result: direct MMIO writes are bandwidth-bound and reach
PCIe line rate; MMIO reads are latency-bound (one transaction per
access) and lose to the copy engine. Correct policy is WC writes via
BAR1 and DMA for reads. The ImDisk reference was a user-mode block
proxy, so ratios are indicative, not an apples-to-apples transport test.
